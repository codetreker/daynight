# Agent Note: Project Management Panel Headings

Status: implemented

## Problem

The ownership and deletion panels repeat general explanations beneath their titles, adding reading overhead before the owner details and available actions.

## Decision

The Project management panels show their titles without subtitles. Owner details, permission explanations, transfer preview, and deletion impact and retention warnings remain in their existing locations.

## Alternatives

**Keep the subtitles.** They state general rules before the actions but repeat information that is available in the relevant permission and confirmation flows.

## Consequences

The panels are shorter while the operation-specific information remains available when needed. The headings no longer provide a general summary of inheritance or deletion scope.
